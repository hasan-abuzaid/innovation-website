About the club

Different backgrounds.
Shared curiosity.

Innovation Club brings students from across German Jordanian University together. Engineers work with designers, business students meet technologists, and ideas get a chance to grow.

Our club has 25+ current members. Our Media, Public Relations, Logistics, and Secretary teams help organize talks, workshops, competitions, and visits beyond campus.

Through our partnership with DI-TECH at GJU, we connect classroom learning with people and experiences from the wider world.

What we do

Six ways to get involved.

Learn something new. Meet someone interesting. Build something that matters.

01

Expert on Campus

Meet founders, engineers, and industry leaders. Ask questions and hear what they have learned.

02

Industry Field Trips

Visit factories, startups, heritage sites, and innovation hubs across Jordan.

03

Hackathons & Competitions

Work with students from different fields to solve real challenges and test your ideas.

04

Technical Workshops

Build practical skills in technology, design thinking, and prototyping.

05

Startup Support

Connect student ideas with mentors and resources through our DI-TECH partnership.

06

Cross-Faculty Network

Meet people beyond your own faculty and find new ways to work together.
