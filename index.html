◆ About the Club

GJU's Engine for Innovation

The Innovation Club at German Jordanian University is the largest and most active student organization on campus, uniting over 1,500 members from every faculty. Born from a shared belief that great ideas don't belong to any single discipline, we've built a space where engineers collaborate with designers, business students build with technologists, and ambition meets execution.

Powered by a 20-person operational board — spanning Media, PR, Logistics, and Secretary functions — the club runs with the discipline of a startup and the energy of a community. Every program we run, every expert we bring to campus, every field trip we organize is purposefully designed to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world impact.

As the flagship student partner of DI-TECH @ GJU, our Expert on Campus (EoC) series has become the most recognized student-organized speaker program at the university — bringing founders, engineers, and industry leaders directly to our students for honest, unfiltered dialogue.

"Innovation isn't a department — it's a mindset. We build the environment where that mindset becomes instinct."

◆ What We Do

Six Pillars of Impact

Everything we do is built around one goal: turning students into people who can actually build things.

01
Flagship

Expert on Campus

Our signature speaker series. Real operators — founders, engineers, executives — sharing real lessons. No slides of theory, just direct dialogue with people who've done it.

02
Immersion

Industry Field Trips

Curated visits to factories, startups, cultural sites, and innovation hubs across Jordan. Seeing the ecosystem firsthand changes how students think about their own potential role in it.

03
Build

Hackathons & Competitions

Intensive building sprints where cross-disciplinary teams tackle real challenges. 100+ students, cash prizes, and direct feedback from industry judges — pressure-tested creativity.

04
Skill

Technical Workshops

Hands-on sessions covering emerging technologies, design thinking, prototyping, and practical skills that GJU curricula haven't yet caught up to.

05
Incubate

Startup Support

Connecting student ventures with mentors and resources through our DI-TECH partnership. Where ideas that survive hackathons get the support to keep going.

06
Community

Cross-Faculty Network

Breaking down faculty silos is itself a program. The most interesting things happen at intersections — we create the conditions for those intersections to exist and thrive.

◆ Programs

What We Run

Three flagship formats. Each one purposefully designed for depth, not breadth.

Flagship Series

Expert on Campus (EoC)

Industry Leaders × GJU Students

Bringing founders, engineers, and industry leaders to share unfiltered insights. Each session curated for depth — one expert, one topic, real conversation. No corporate presentations; just direct access to people building things that matter.

Format Talk + Live Q&A
Frequency Bi-weekly
Venue GJU Campus
Competitions

Hackathons & Innovation Challenges

Build. Compete. Win.

Multi-day events where 100+ students form cross-disciplinary teams, compete for cash prizes, and get direct feedback from industry judges. The most intense and most rewarding thing we run.

Format 2-Day Sprint
Prize Pool 500+ JD
Field Immersion

Industry & Heritage Trails

Beyond the campus walls

Curated field visits to industrial facilities, cultural heritage sites, and entrepreneurial ecosystems across Jordan — As-Salt, Madaba, Amman's tech hubs. Because the best classroom is the world itself.

Type Full-Day Trips
Scope National
