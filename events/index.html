Hackathon

Passionate Innovators

A two-day hackathon where 100 students worked through hands-on challenges and shared their ideas. Teams competed for a 500 JD prize, with collaboration and creative problem-solving at the heart of the event.

Expert on Campus

EoC: Hisham Abubaker

The CEO and founder of Dimitri's Coffee and Kanaan IoT joined us for a conversation about industry and the market. Students had the chance to ask questions and learn directly from his experience.

Field trip · Madaba

Madaba Innovation Trail

A visit connecting cultural heritage with industry. Students explored archaeological sites and industrial facilities in Madaba, looking at how history and economic development can inform new ideas.

Conference partnership · 2025

Industrial Partnership Conference 2025

We partnered with DI-TECH to organize IPC 2025 at GJU under the patronage of Prof. Ala'aldeen Alhalhouli. Our team supported logistics, media, guest lists, and event coordination.

Expert on Campus

EoC: Eng. Salah Aloqabi

As part of Expert on Campus, Eng. Salah Aloqabi shared his industry experience and market insights. The session gave students a chance to ask questions and connect their studies with professional practice.
